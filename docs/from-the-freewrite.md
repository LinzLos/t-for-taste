# From the freewrite: what slop is, and the taste-idea family

Assembled 2026-09-05 from Lindsay's April–June 2026 freewrite. Her phrases are kept where they are the point; this is source material for captions and posts, not copy.

## Slop is a missing decision

Read together, the freewrite has one idea about AI slop: it is output with no author for its small choices. Every line below names a decision nobody made.

1. **Slop is extra.** "copious amounts of EXTRA ai brings to the table its overload a waste … I'm sure this could have been a lot shorter and I want human." The shortest definition.
2. **Slop is polish nobody decided.** "Who at Anthropic is telling the model how to compute 'a tiny scootch'??"
3. **Slop is lift-and-reskin.** "you literally can use AI to lift any mobile desktop app you want reskin and ship?? what does it even mean to ship product any more." When everything can be lifted, only the decisions are yours.
4. **Slop is a tool pretending to be a being.** "it's not sentient it's a tool, like spell check, like a car, like a pacemaker. why don't people treat it like a tool?" And the irony of being "token conscious."
5. **Slop is help that isn't help.** The health-system AI that pitches a medic-alert discount before letting you pay the bill; the sandwich ad injected at checkout. "AI is exposing how loosely we've been using the word 'help'."
6. **Slop is solving the solved.** "solutions to problems that are already solved."
7. **The loop closes.** "human mimicking the way ai." Pinocchio, who cannot lie and wants to be a real boy.

**The cure, in her words:** "aspirational aesthetic weights was an issue I had to operationalize … taste based routines this has to happen." One-liner: "a design without insight is posture."

Two phrases from the same week, same shape: **"AI away spray"** (slop as visual sameness, the reason the series refuses default UI) and **"fails successfully"** (a drag that lands beautifully while the Slack message never sends).

This is why the caption format is Default / Change / Reason with a real number. It is not a description of the episode. It is the decision, shown.
